### Registration

To create a new account:

1.  Navigate to the **Register** page.
2.  Enter your **Name**.
3.  Enter a valid **Email** address.
4.  Create a **Password** and confirm it in the **Repeat password** field.
5.  Click the **Register** button to create your account.

![Registration Page](assets/register.png)
_Registration Page_

### Login

To access your account:

1.  Navigate to the **Login** page.
2.  Enter your **Email** and **Password**.
3.  Click **Sign In**.
4.  If you don't have an account, click **Register** to switch to the registration page.
